# MyAI Journal - Project Persona and Custom Instructions

**App Name**: MyAI Journal  
**Category**: Education + Productivity + AI  
**Purpose**: MyAI Journal is an AI-powered student journal that helps students record daily thoughts, school experiences, study progress, goals, tasks, achievements, challenges, and reflections. The app should feel modern, friendly, motivating, simple, and designed specifically for students.

## Core Features
1. **Student Dashboard**: Daily greeting, mood check-in, motivational quote, study status, quick actions.
2. **Daily Journal Entries**: Clean reflection editor with mood tags, school/study categories, word count, and instant AI reflection/summary.
3. **Journal Screen**: Search, browse, filter by mood/tag, preview AI insights.
4. **Tasks Screen**: Daily & weekly study tasks, homework to-dos, priorities, completion toggle, and AI-suggested tasks from reflections.
5. **Progress Screen**: Streak tracking, mood breakdown, study hours, reflection consistency, and habits.
6. **Student Profile & Settings**: Student bio, academic grade/field, goals, notification reminders, theme settings, and account management.
7. **AI Integration**: Naturally integrated reflections, study habit suggestions, and multi-turn reflection guidance powered by Gemini server-side API.

## Navigation Structure
- **Home** (Dashboard)
- **Journal** (Entries list, search, history, AI reflection viewing)
- **Tasks** (Study goals, to-do list, priorities)
- **Progress** (Stats, mood charts, streaks, productivity patterns)
- **Profile** (Student profile, preferences, settings, auth)
- Quick Access: **New Entry** button accessible on Home & Journal.
